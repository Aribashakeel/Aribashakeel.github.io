About

Applied mathematics across biological and physical systems

I am a PhD researcher in Applied Mathematics at the University of Birmingham, working under the supervision of Dr Meurig Gallagher and Professor David J. Smith .

My research interests span mathematical biology, mathematical modelling, applied differential equations, asymptotic analysis and scientific computation. My current work focuses on physiological modelling, while my previous research was in fluid dynamics and boundary-layer theory.

01
Current Research

Mathematical Modelling to Understand the Treatment of Adrenal Insufficiency

My PhD research develops a mathematical modelling toolkit to investigate cortisol replacement therapy in adrenal insufficiency. The models incorporate physiological mechanisms including cortisol–protein binding, cortisol–cortisone interconversion, tissue distribution and cortisol excretion.

The aim is to investigate different dosing strategies and understand the mechanisms underlying cortisol dynamics and inter-patient variability.

Previous Research

Fluid Dynamics & Boundary-Layer Theory

My previous research focused on rotating flow, boundary-layer theory and computational fluid mechanics. My MSc dissertation investigated rotating flows over a stationary plane surface.

I subsequently worked as a Research Assistant on mathematical and numerical modelling of MHD boundary-layer flow with variable fluid properties.
