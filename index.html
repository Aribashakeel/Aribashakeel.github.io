About

I am currently undertaking doctoral research in Applied Mathematics at the University of Birmingham. My research lies at the interface of mathematics, biology and medicine, with an emphasis on understanding physiological processes through mathematical models.

I am particularly interested in how mathematical analysis and computational methods can be used to understand treatment strategies and the underlying mechanisms governing biological systems.

Research

My research develops mechanistic mathematical models of cortisol dynamics, including hormone transport, protein binding and hydrocortisone administration. I use analytical and computational approaches to investigate model behaviour and its physiological interpretation.
