About

I am a PhD researcher in Applied Mathematics at the University of Birmingham. My research focuses on mathematical modelling of physiological systems, with particular interest in cortisol dynamics and hydrocortisone replacement therapy.

Research Interests

My research interests include mathematical biology, mechanistic modelling, asymptotic analysis, parameter estimation, identifiability, pharmacokinetic modelling, and mathematical approaches to physiological systems.
