About Me

Applied Mathematics & Mathematical Biology

I am a PhD researcher in Applied Mathematics at the University of Birmingham, working under the supervision of Dr Meurig Gallagher and Professor David J. Smith .

My research lies at the interface of applied mathematics, mathematical biology and physiology. I develop and analyse mechanistic mathematical models to understand cortisol dynamics and hydrocortisone replacement therapy in adrenal insufficiency.
