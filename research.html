Current Research

Mathematical Modelling to Understand the Treatment of Adrenal Insufficiency

My PhD research develops a mathematical modelling toolkit to investigate cortisol replacement therapy in adrenal insufficiency.

The models incorporate key physiological mechanisms, including cortisol–protein binding, cortisol–cortisone interconversion, tissue distribution and cortisol excretion. The aim is to explore different dosing strategies and understand the physiological mechanisms underlying cortisol dynamics and inter-patient variability.

Previous Research

Fluid Dynamics & Boundary-Layer Theory

My previous research focused on fluid dynamics, boundary-layer theory, rotating flow and computational fluid mechanics.

My MSc dissertation at the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST) was titled Investigation of rotating flows over a stationary plane surface .

I subsequently worked as a Research Assistant on modelling MHD fluid flow over a stretching wedge using variable fluid properties.
